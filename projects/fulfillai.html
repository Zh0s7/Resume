Problem and architecture

The platform investigates coordinated material transport and order fulfillment across a fleet of autonomous robots. A Python simulation engine advances discrete ticks and manages robot state, movement, tasks, charging, and repeatable scenarios.

Custom A* pathfinding and space-time reservations coordinate movement by reserving positions over time. Task allocation, traffic management, pickup, delivery, and replanning connect navigation to warehouse operations.

Persistent operations

Orders progress through creation, inventory reservation, robot allocation, navigation, pickup, delivery, completion, and persistent inventory updates. FastAPI and PostgreSQL maintain authoritative state, with Redis Streams and a transactional outbox supporting event-driven processing.

A React, TypeScript, and Three.js frontend visualizes robots, inventory, orders, and system state. Docker Compose hosts the application and infrastructure, including a verified native ARM64 deployment.

Limitations and next work

Orders currently contain one SKU. General liveness under all congestion conditions is unproven, full-route battery forecasting is absent, and sustained end-to-end operation at 20 Hz remains unverified.

Saturated 100-robot API recovery took 5.47 seconds against a two-second target. Authentication, retention, and production infrastructure hardening remain incomplete. Zero detected invariant violations in a scenario does not establish a universal collision or deadlock guarantee.

iPod Classic

I installed the Rockbox bootloader and replacement operating system on a seventh-generation iPod Classic. The setup provides filesystem-based music management, drag-and-drop storage, FLAC and lossless support, DSP, equalization, and an alternative interface.

The customization succeeded technically. Practical evaluation found manually maintaining a local library less convenient for my use than streaming, informing the project’s usability assessment.

Nintendo DSi XL

I customized the DSi XL with TWiLight Menu++, homebrew integration, and SD-card application execution. The work involved embedded boot architecture and legacy ARM9/ARM7 hardware.

Planned original contribution

I intend to investigate Apple wired-earbud remote buttons on the seventh-generation iPod Classic and develop suitable Rockbox input-event support.

Hardware compatibility research, event handling, validation, and upstream review are planned. An original contribution has yet to be implemented, submitted, or accepted.

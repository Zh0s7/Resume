Team objective

Team Phantom develops an electric formula-style competition vehicle. My role is within the vehicle dynamics engineering team, contributing to a shared design-build-test process.

Engineering workstreams

My work involves mechanical CAD, steering and suspension research, structural simulation, mechanical component development, fabrication and assembly, and vehicle testing considerations.

Sensor placement and instrumentation planning connect mechanical design with physical measurements. Development data can inform subsequent vehicle iterations.

Individual scope and documentation

The vehicle is the product of the engineering team. Specific component ownership, fabrication methods, structural simulation results, and quantitative test outcomes require further documentation.

Tools and methods

SolidWorks, mechanical CAD, FEA, suspension and steering geometry, structural analysis, manufacturing considerations, instrumentation, data acquisition, and experimental validation.

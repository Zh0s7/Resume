Honda D15B2 Engine Rebuild

Independent teardown, metrology, and mechanical reassembly

A full independent rebuild of an inline-four SOHC engine, including dimensional inspection, wear assessment, and component replacement.

Disassembly and inspection

I independently disassembled the Honda D15B2, inspecting the block, head, valvetrain, pistons, connecting rods, crankshaft, seals, lubrication system, cooling system, and mechanical interfaces.

Dimensional and tolerance checks informed evaluation of component wear and suitability for reuse. Specific measured values and inspection instruments are awaiting original documentation.

Failure analysis and rebuild

I examined damaged or unsuitable components, replaced parts as required, and completed mechanical reassembly. The work connected wear, lubrication, clearances, and component condition to assembly decisions.

The project involved automotive hand tools, power tools, and measurement equipment, with precision assembly and troubleshooting throughout.

Validation boundaries

The mechanical rebuild is complete. Installed operation, compression testing, and dyno performance remain undocumented.

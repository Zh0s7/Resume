JobPilot

Agentic workflow automation and application management

A local automation platform joining modular job discovery, configurable ranking, document generation, browser workflows, and human approval.

Discovery and data processing

Modular adapters integrate or have validated sources including Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Remotive, and structured public postings. Normalization, deduplication, first-publication detection, freshness checks, and eligibility filters prepare ranked shortlists.

Multiplicative ranking weights account for employer preference, title relevance, technical fit, geography, eligibility, compensation, and user preferences. Especially desirable opportunities can bypass ordinary geographic filters.

Document and browser workflows

LLM-assisted tailoring combines job descriptions with a master résumé while preserving its template. The workflow includes LaTeX and PDF generation, inspection, prompt versioning, and human approval.

Playwright supports form interaction, pause/resume, application state, approval timeouts, and intervention. Discord provides notifications and approvals. React dashboards support review, ranking preferences, batch approvals, and tracking, alongside Excel integration.

Validation context

The metrics below describe separate development runs and benchmarks. They reflect development evidence rather than production-wide reliability.

Known limitations

Tailoring quality and dashboard ranking-preference synchronization need refinement. LinkedIn and Indeed implementation status, remote browser takeover, and fully autonomous real-world submission remain unconfirmed. Production-scale reliability needs further validation.
